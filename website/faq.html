Questions? Answers.

Short answers to the things people ask most.

Is it free?

Yes. Syntaz and Syntaz Lite are free. The Gemini key from Google is free too.

Can I use ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Groq or Hugging Face instead?

Yes, in the full Syntaz: choose OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), xAI (Grok), Groq or Hugging Face when you set it up, or later with Change AI in the Syntaz window. You need your own key from that company, and you pay them for what Syntaz uses (usually a few cents per task). Syntaz never sees your bill. Set a monthly spending limit in your account to be safe. The chat on this website and Syntaz Lite use Gemini only.

Which Hugging Face token do I need?

A Fine-grained token with just one permission: under Inference, tick Make calls to Inference Providers. Leave everything else off. The button in Syntaz's setup opens the token page with that already ticked: name it, click Create token, copy it (it starts with hf_) and paste it into setup.

Can Syntaz make pictures?

Yes, if your AI can: OpenAI, xAI (Grok), Hugging Face, and Google Gemini with billing turned on (Gemini's free tier makes no pictures). Press the 🎨 button, or just ask "draw a cat in space", and download it. Claude, Groq and LM Studio can't make pictures, and Syntaz tells you so. Pictures usually cost more than messages.

Does Syntaz work on Linux?

Yes. Open a terminal and run curl -fsSL https://ben10-games.github.io/Syntaz-AI/install.sh | bash. It downloads Syntaz into a Syntaz AI folder in your home folder and opens the setup window. No sudo needed.

What is the LTS version?

LTS means long-term support: a stable version of Syntaz that only gets fixes, no new features. Choose it if you want Syntaz to stay the same. Download it on the Download page, or switch any time under Updates in the Syntaz window.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. With free Google Gemini, Syntaz works even on slow computers. Only LM Studio (running the AI on your own computer) needs a strong graphics card.

What's the daily limit?

Gemini's free tier allows a limited number of requests a day, roughly 100 tasks with the default model. It resets every day.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me?

Syntaz doesn't come from an app store, so your computer double-checks. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On Mac, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Can it do my homework for me?

It explains things and helps you work through them. You stay in charge, and it doesn't hand anything in by itself.

How do I update?

Open the Syntaz window and click Update. One click and you have the newest version.

How do I uninstall?

Open the Syntaz window and click Uninstall.

Does it work on Chromebooks?

Syntaz Lite does. It's one file that opens in any browser, with nothing to install. Get Syntaz Lite
