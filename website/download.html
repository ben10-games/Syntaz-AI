Add the extension to Chrome

  1. 1Unzip Syntaz-Chrome-Extension.zip. You get a Syntaz AI folder. Keep it somewhere it can stay (for example in Documents).
  2. 2In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. 3Click Load unpacked and pick the Syntaz AI folder.
  4. 4Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Syntaz AI. Click it (or press Alt+N) to open Syntaz next to any page.
  5. 5Open ⚙ Settings, choose your AI and paste its key (get a free one), then click Save & check. Done!

Updating: unzip the new version over the old folder, then click ↻ on Syntaz in chrome://extensions. Your key and settings stay.

No Developer mode? School and work Chromebooks often block it. Use the website chat, the desktop app or Syntaz Lite instead.

Get your free Gemini key

The key lets Syntaz use Google's free AI. It stays in your browser. Prefer another AI? Syntaz also takes OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), xAI (Grok), Groq and Hugging Face keys (you pay them), or your own AI server (LM Studio, Ollama).

Open Google AI Studio
  1. 1Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2Click Create API key.
  3. 3Copy the key and paste it into Syntaz.
